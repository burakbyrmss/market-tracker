Market Tracker shows cryptocurrency and currency prices. It has no accounts, no ads, and no analytics. The app does not ask for your name, email address, location, contacts, or any other personal information.

## Data stored on your device

Your settings (theme, language, display currency), your watchlists, and recently loaded prices and rates are kept only on your device so the app works offline. They are never sent to us or anyone else. Deleting the app deletes them.

## Services the app contacts

To show prices, the app connects directly from your device to these services. Like any internet connection, each one receives your IP address and basic connection data, under its own privacy policy. The app sends them no personal information.

| Service                                                                                  | Why                              |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------- |
| Binance (`binance.vision`)                                                               | Cryptocurrency prices and charts |
| Kraken (`kraken.com`), used where Binance is not available                               | Cryptocurrency prices and charts |
| Frankfurter (`frankfurter.dev`), which republishes European Central Bank reference rates | Currency exchange rates          |
| Expo (`u.expo.dev`)                                                                      | Checks for app updates           |
| Sentry (`sentry.io`)                                                                     | Crash reports (see below)        |

## Crash reports

When the app crashes, it sends a crash report to Sentry so we can fix the bug. A report contains the error and where in the code it happened, the app version, the device model, the operating system version, and the steps inside the app just before the crash, such as which screen was open. It contains no name, account, or identifier for you, and is not used for analytics or advertising. Reports are kept for TODO days (Sentry plan retention).

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone.
